# Referenzboss-Grafik

Erstellt mit dem eingebauten Imagegen-Werkzeug. Endgültig im Projekt verwendet: `frontend/public/ships/bosses/insect/viridian-harvester.webp` (1659×948, echte Alpha-Transparenz, 379332 Byte). PNG-Ausgangsbild separat erhalten. Nur nach WebP konvertiert; keine Spiegelung, Umfärbung oder erfundene Waffentextur. Waffen werden unabhängig mit dem bestehenden Atlas gerendert. 20 normale Gegnersilhouetten sind neue, im Spiel einmal dekodierte SVG-Designs, keine KI-Rasterbilder.

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production top-down 2D game sprite with high-detail realistic 3D rendered materials, transparent RGBA background. Create ONE bilaterally symmetric alien insect-inspired science-fiction capital warship called Viridian Harvester for Cryptoid Evolution, front points DOWN, rear up, perfectly overhead orthographic view, no perspective foreshortening. Wide compact silhouette approximately 2.4 times wider than tall. It is an advanced MECHANICAL SPACECRAFT, inspired by a praying mantis and hornet, never an animal or literal insect. Dark anthracite machined metal chitin armor plates with razor-edged segmented side wings, short curved mandible-like forward forks, acid-green luminous energy veins and orange inner reactor slit. Strong convincing 3D depth, beveled metal, subtle surface wear, aggressive toxic neon-green and acid-yellow highlights. Four circular MECHANICAL turret sockets: outer left and right smaller sockets on wings and inner left and right larger sockets. IMPORTANT: sockets are empty mounting discs, do NOT paint cannon barrels or guns because rotating mechanical cannons will be rendered independently by the game. Central recessed CLOSED reactor bay, no text. Keep all projections short, no legs extending far downward. Ship occupies 90% image width and roughly 65% image height. Crisp fully isolated cutout, genuine transparent background, no cast shadow outside the hull, no starfield, no panel, no labels, no watermark, no extra objects. Premium realistic space arcade asset, not cartoon.
